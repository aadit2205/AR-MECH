Point. Read. Turn.What every knob does

Aim the camera at the amplifier. The boombox sticker on the big volume knob anchors the guide, and every control gets named where it actually sits.

Tracking locks onto the sticker's colours — cyan body, yellow 90'S. Everything else is drawn in proportion to it. Once locked, the guide treats the panel as fixed on the wall: swing the camera away and it follows your phone's turn off-screen, then finds its way back onto the soundbox the moment you turn back — it only freezes in place if your phone has no tilt sensor to track with.
